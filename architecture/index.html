The Traditional Bottleneck
  • •Incomplete Information: Panicked 911 callers omit critical location or hazard data.
  • •Sequential Phone Trees: Dispatchers make separate calls to EMS, Fire, and Hospitals.
  • •Unclear Severity: Trapped victims and fluid leaks only discovered once units arrive on scene.
  • •Siloed Visibility: Responders do not share the same real-time situational picture.
The AI Coordination Layer
  • •Faster Information Flow: Instant extraction from voice waveforms and photos.
  • •Better Resource Coordination: Automated recommendation of ALS vs BLS units.
  • •Shared Tactical Picture: Real-time updates shared across all responding agency dashboards.
  • •Connected Ecosystem: ER doctors receive incoming patient vitals before the ambulance arrives.
